# AI assistance in Pyright contributions

You may use AI tools to learn about Pyright and help develop a contribution. The person submitting the
contribution is responsible for all of it, including any AI-generated material.

- **Write public contributions in your own words.** Write the substantive text of issues, pull request
  descriptions, reviews, and comments yourself. If you include AI-generated text, clearly label it and
  provide your own explanation rather than relying on the generated text to speak for you. If you use
  AI to translate your writing, include your original text alongside the labeled translation.
- **Review and own the entire contribution.** Before submitting, personally review the full change,
  make sure you understand and can explain it, and run the checks appropriate to the change. Take
  responsibility for addressing review feedback and correcting problems that come up after submission.
- **Keep submissions focused and manageable.** Avoid filing issues, posting comments, or opening pull
  requests in bulk. Split large changes into focused pull requests when practical.
- **Keep a human in charge.** Pyright does not accept contributions from autonomous AI agents. You
  may use AI to explore the codebase or learn how to tackle a `good first issue`, but do not hand the
  issue to a tool and submit its solution without doing the work yourself.

Adapted from [Pyrefly's AI contribution policy](https://github.com/pyrefly-org/pyrefly/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md).
